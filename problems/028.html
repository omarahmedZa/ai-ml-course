Write masked_nll(log_probs, mask) returning the mean negative log-probability over the positions that are masked.

  • log_probs[i] is the natural-log probability the model gave the true token at position i
  • mask[i] is truthy only for the positions the objective scores
  • unmasked positions contribute nothing, not even to the denominator
  • raise ValueError when the lengths differ, or when no position is masked
  • return a float

This is the average a masked objective takes: the 15% that are hidden, not all 100% of the window.
