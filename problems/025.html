surrogate(ratio, advantage) is the unclipped policy-gradient objective.

Add clipped_surrogate(ratio, advantage, epsilon=0.2) returning PPO's objective min(r * A, clip(r, 1 - epsilon, 1 + epsilon) * A).

  • the clip is two-sided: 1 - epsilon below, 1 + epsilon above
  • raise ValueError when epsilon is outside [0, 1] or ratio <= 0
  • return a float

Do not change surrogate. For a bad action with a negative advantage the lower clip is the binding one, so the objective can be *worse* than r * A.
