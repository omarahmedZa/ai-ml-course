ks_statistic(a, b) should return the two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic: the largest absolute gap between the empirical CDFs of the two samples.

The starter compares paired sorted values, which is not a CDF and breaks when the samples have different lengths. Fix it. Raise ValueError if either sample is empty. Do not mutate the inputs, and the result must be symmetric in a and b.

The contract

def ks_statistic(a, b):

Starter

def ks_statistic(a, b):
    """Return the two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic."""
    xs, ys = sorted(a), sorted(b)
    return max(abs(x - y) for x, y in zip(xs, ys))
