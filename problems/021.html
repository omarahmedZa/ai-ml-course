Write epsilon_greedy_probs(q_values, epsilon) returning the probability of each action under epsilon-greedy.

- with probability 1 - epsilon play a greedy action; split that mass evenly among tied best actions - with probability epsilon pick uniformly at random over all k actions - the returned list must have one probability per action and sum to 1

Raise ValueError for an empty q_values or an epsilon outside [0, 1]. Return floats. For epsilon = 0.2 and four arms, the greedy arm gets 0.85.
