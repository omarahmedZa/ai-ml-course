Write dropout(values, p, mask) applying one dropout mask, as in training.

  • a True entry in mask keeps the activation, divided by 1 - p
  • a False entry returns 0.0
  • raise ValueError unless 0 <= p < 1
  • raise ValueError when mask and values differ in length
  • return floats and do not mutate the inputs

Inference needs no rescale, because the correction is paid during training.
