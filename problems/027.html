Write block_parameters(d_model, n_heads, expansion=4) counting matrix weights in one pre-LN Transformer block.

  • attention projections: 4 * d_model ** 2 (Q, K, V and output)
  • feed-forward: 2 * expansion * d_model ** 2 (up and down)
  • return {"attention": ..., "ffn": ..., "total": ...} with integer counts

Raise ValueError when any argument is not a positive integer, or when d_model is not divisible by n_heads. Biases and embeddings are not counted, so the head count never changes the total: at d_model=512 the total is 3,145,728.
