Fairness gaps from a decision table

Write bias_gaps(y_true, y_pred, groups) returning a dict with two floats:

  • demographic_parity: the largest selection rate minus the smallest, where a group's selection rate is the share of its rows with y_pred == 1
  • equal_opportunity: the same gap computed over true-positive rates, where a group's TPR is the share predicted 1 among rows with y_true == 1

Groups with no positive labels are skipped when computing equal opportunity; if fewer than two groups remain, report 0.0 for it.

Raise ValueError when the three sequences differ in length or fewer than two distinct groups are present. Do not mutate the inputs.
