The objective, one symbol at a time · Lab 1

θ — the numbers that move

You can say which numbers training changes and which it can never touch.

Where this sits

θ* = argmin θ (1/n) Σi L( fθ(xi), yi )

Lab 1 of 9 · the whole expression is dimmed; only θ is lit

θ (theta) is one symbol standing for a whole list of numbers a model learns. The formula says: try every candidate θ, and keep the one whose average loss is smallest. Nothing else in that expression can move.

Two kinds of number

An equation mixes two things that look identical on the page: quantities you supply, and quantities the model works out. Training only touches the second kind.

NumberWho sets itChanges in training?
xiThe dataset. You collect it: here 0, 2, 4.No
yiThe dataset. You label it: here 1, 5, 9.No
θ (w and b)Training. It starts as a guess and is nudged.Yes

xi (x-sub-i) is the i-th example, the input you measured. yi (y-sub-i) is its true answer, the number you want predicted. Both arrive with the dataset and stay put. θ is the only mover: the weight w (slope) and the bias b (where the line crosses the y-axis).

The line is θ; the dots are data

Three points, one line through them. Slide the two numbers and watch which of the two moves.

The sliders are θ. Drag w away from 2 and the line tilts; drag b away from 1 and the line lifts or sinks. Nothing else on the plot is under your control.

The trap

Most common wrong mental model

"The model learned the data." It feels right because after training the predictions match the rows, so the rows seem to have been absorbed. That is the output of learning, not its content.

Correction: training stored θ and nothing else. The rows were read, scored and then left where they were. With w = 2, b = 1 the model reproduces 1, 5, 9 without holding any of them.

The tell: add a row and no stored number changes on its own. The dataset is now bigger, so the average loss is a different number — yet θ stays exactly where it was until you run training again.

Retrieval check

  1. For y = w·x + b, how many numbers are in θ?
    Answer

    Two: w and b. The symbol θ is a bag holding both, which is why it is bolded or written as a list whenever the count matters.

  2. You add ten rows to the dataset and do not retrain. What changes?
    Answer

    The loss changes, because the average now runs over more examples. θ does not change: w and b are untouched until training runs again.
