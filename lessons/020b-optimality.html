Value functions, one symbol at a time · Lab 2

max — the value of the best action

You can turn a row of action scores into one state value, and name the action the max selects.

Where this sits

V*(s) = maxa Q(s, a)

Topic 20 · the state value is lit; the row of action scores it reads stays dim

Q(s,a) scores one action in one state: the return you expect if you commit to a now, then behave well afterwards. V(s) scores the state. The bridge between the two is a maximum.

This is the smallest equation in the unit and the one that turns evaluation into control. An average tells you how good your current habit is; a maximum tells you how good the best available move is.

Best action, best state

The max runs over the actions legal in s. With Q(left) = 2.8, Q(right) = 1.5 and Q(wait) = 2.2, the state is worth 2.8 and the policy picks left. The losing scores are not deleted — they are what you compare when the estimates move. Exact ties need a tie-break rule, and they are rare once the scores come from samples.

Read the crossings: left wins at states 3, 5 and 6; right wins at 1, 2 and 4. A value built by averaging the two curves would sit between them and describe neither policy.

The max sits inside the target

In Q*(s,a) = r + γ maxa′ Q*(s′,a′), the maximum is taken at the next state, not the current one. That placement is what lets one-step greedy choices add up to a long-horizon plan: every score already contains the best continuation from there.

Two policies, one gap

Same state, two behaviours. Always-left scores 2.8. A policy taking left 70% of the time and right 30% scores 0.7 × 2.8 + 0.3 × 1.5 = 2.41. The gap of 0.39 is the price of hesitating, and the max is the operation that removes it.

The trap

Most common wrong mental model

"Greedy means short-sighted." Greedy on the wrong numbers is short-sighted. Greedy on Q* is optimal, because every Q* already contains the future through the max in its own target.

Correction: the danger is not the max, it is the estimate the max reads. A maximum over noisy scores reliably picks the luckiest noise, not the best action.

The tell: the chosen action flips after one new sample, or the 0.3 margin moves by more than 0.3 when you re-run. The max is innocent; the table underneath it has not settled.

Retrieval check

  1. Q(left) = 2.8 and Q(right) = 1.5. What is V*(s), and what does the agent do?
    Answer

    V*(s) = 2.8 and the policy takes left. The right value still matters: it is the comparison that makes 2.8 the maximum.

  2. Where does the max apply in Q*(s,a) = r + γ maxa′ Q*(s′,a′)?
    Answer

    At the next state s′, over its actions a′. The current action is the one you are scoring, not one you are choosing.
