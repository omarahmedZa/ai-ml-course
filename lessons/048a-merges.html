Topic 48 · the whole learner is one line, run until the vocabulary budget is full

A symbol is any string already in the vocabulary; at the start, one character. The rule counts every adjacent pair across the corpus, replaces the winner with one new symbol, and starts again. The saved merge list — in order — is the tokenizer.

Count, merge, recount

Take a corpus of nine words: sun five times, sung three times, gun twice. As characters that is 5 × 3 + 3 × 4 + 2 × 3 = 33 symbols and 23 adjacent pairs.

Counting by copies gives (u,n) 10, (s,u) 8, (n,g) 3 and (g,u) 2. The winner is u+n, so all ten occurrences become the symbol un. Press play, or drag the scrubber, to advance one merge at a time; the number beside each pair is its count before that merge.

The merge is not a subtraction. When u+n becomes un, every pair that touched either half changes identity: (s,u), (u,n), (n,g) and (g,u) all leave the list. New pairs appear at the joins — (s,un) and (un,g) — and (g,u) returns as (g,un), still counted twice but no longer the same pair. The counts 8, 3 and 2 all belong to pairs that did not exist a moment earlier.

The vocabulary is four characters, s, u, n, g, plus the four merged symbols, so eight. The corpus holds ten token positions because the same symbol is reused. Vocabulary counts distinct labels; tokens count positions.

The trap— merges

Most common wrong mental model

"A merge only changes the pair it merges." It changes every pair that touched either half. u+n removes (s,u) and (n,g) as well as (u,n), and the count that was (g,u) returns under the new pair (g,un).

Correction: after each merge, rebuild the adjacency list. The counts are not the old table minus the winner; the pair identities change too.

The tell: your count table stops adding up — the total no longer matches the number of adjacencies. With 33 symbols in nine words there are 23 pairs; after one merge 13 remain, and none of the three survivors existed before.

Retrieval check— merges

  1. Why does (s,u) disappear after the first merge, even though no s or u character was deleted?
    Answer

    A pair is two adjacent symbols. The u now sits inside the symbol un, which has one left edge and one right edge, so the old s–u boundary is gone. The s now forms the pair (s,un).

  2. The corpus ends at 10 tokens with a vocabulary of 8 symbols. Why are those two numbers different?
    Answer

    The vocabulary is the set of distinct symbols — four characters and four merges. The 10 counts positions: sun appears five times, sung three, gun two. Repeated symbols share one vocabulary entry.
