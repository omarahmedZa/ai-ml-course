# Mission: pass the AI/ML course, then build something that runs

## Why
I am examined on eight areas — ML, DL, NLP, RL, DRL, Attention/LLM, Explainable AI, Federated Learning — and I want a project that uses them. Knowing the material is worth nothing to me unless it survives an exam question and a real build.

## Success looks like
- **Explain:** for any of the 35 topics, 90 seconds, no notes, correct mechanism, one concrete example.
- **Solve:** exam-style problems by hand — dot products, gradients, attention weights, Bellman backups, FedAvg — and diagnose a failing model from its symptoms.
- **Build:** one shipped project containing a model I trained or fine-tuned, a short explainability report, and a one-paragraph data-locality/privacy argument.

## Constraints
- Beginner: no ML coursework. Comfortable with code.
- Math is rusty — linear algebra, calculus, probability get rebuilt in Unit 0, from zero.
- ~3 weeks, 6–8 h/week.
- One topic per session. 8–12 minutes of reading each.

## Out of scope
- Reproducing papers from scratch.
- Training foundation models.
- MLOps depth, Kubernetes, GPU cluster engineering.
