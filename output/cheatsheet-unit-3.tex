\sect{Regularisers}
Dropout: zero w.p. $p$, rescale $\frac{1}{1-p}$; \textbf{identity at inference}\\
Weight decay: $L+\lambda\|w\|^2$ \quad Early stop: keep best val\\
Augmentation must preserve the label (flip 6 $\leftrightarrow$ 9: no)

\sect{Signal through depth}
gain $g$ over $L$ layers $\Rightarrow g^L$\\
$0.25^{10}=9.5\times10^{-7}$ \quad $1.5^{10}=58$ \quad $1.5^{50}=6.4\times10^{8}$\\
Fixes: He/Xavier init, LayerNorm, residual $y=x+f(x)$, clip, warmup

\sect{Gotchas}
\begin{itemize}
\item BatchNorm at inference uses running stats: call \texttt{eval()}.
\item Depth without residuals can \emph{reduce} trainability.
\item CNN on tabular data: adjacency is arbitrary, prior is false.
\end{itemize}
